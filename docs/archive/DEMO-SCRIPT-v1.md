# Five-minute demonstration

0:00–0:30 — Explain the premise
Open the overview. Explain that self-exclusion should reach participating physical outlets, including laptop-operated shops. Point out the independent-demonstration banner. Nothing here blocks real gambling or contacts a regulator.

0:30–1:15 — Enrol and activate
Select Protect myself. Enter a fictional name, select Lagos, choose self-service or assisted enrolment, enter the displayed mock OTP 246810 and confirm voluntary enrolment. Choose 90 days and consent. Confirm that the protection status shows server-calculated start and end times in WAT.

1:15–2:00 — Check at a physical shop
Generate and copy the five-minute private reference. Open Explore the demo and enter as Shop attendant. Paste the reference and confirm the mock identity check. Check exclusion. Read the warning and record Service refused. Explain that neither assessment responses nor complaint details are visible to the attendant.

2:00–2:30 — Show safe failure
Enable Simulate connection failure and attempt another check. Explain that failure is never treated as approval. This demo does not accept or place bets.

2:30–3:30 — Report a fictional allegation
Switch back to Player; the demo remembers the same fictional player. Submit a report against a fictional Lagos outlet. Show the case number and timeline in My reports. Optional fictional evidence can be attached there.

3:30–4:15 — Review in the right jurisdiction
Switch to State regulator, Lagos. Open Complaints & cases and find the new report. Mark it Under Review and add a note. Point out that Oyo's demo regulator cannot see or update a Lagos case. These are allegations, not findings.

4:15–5:00 — Recovery and next steps
Return as Player and show the case update. Open the educational check-in and public resources. Show the extension action: time can be added, never removed. Close by explaining that real implementation needs approved identity verification, regulator participation, outlet adoption, privacy review and recovery partnerships.

Additional scenarios
- Assisted enrolment: choose the assisted or basic-phone demonstration option at registration; no real SMS/USSD is sent.
- Expiry: seeded completed records and automated boundary tests demonstrate expiry; do not alter a player's clock to attempt to defeat server validation.
- Owner: register a fictional outlet, assign an attendant and review training.
- Admin: inspect audit records and change available future durations. Do not reset during an unfinished presentation.
